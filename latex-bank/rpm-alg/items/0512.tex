\textbf{보기}에서 옳은 것만을 있는 대로 고르시오. \bogi{ㄱ. $\dfrac{1-\cos^2\theta}{\tan^2\theta}+\sin^2\theta=0$\\[1mm] ㄴ. $\left(1+\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\right)\left(1+\dfrac{1}{\cos\theta}\right)$\\ \hspace*{1.2em}$\times\left(1-\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{\cos\theta}\right)=2$\\[1mm] ㄷ. $\left(\sin\theta+\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\right)^2+\left(\cos\theta+\dfrac{1}{\cos\theta}\right)^2$\\ \hspace*{1.2em}$-\left(\tan\theta+\dfrac{1}{\tan\theta}\right)^2=5$}
