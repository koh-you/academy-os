다음은 $1\le r<n$일 때, ${}_n\mathrm{C}_r={}_{n-1}\mathrm{C}_r+{}_{n-1}\mathrm{C}_{r-1}$이 성립함을 증명하는 과정이다. ㈎, ㈏, ㈐에 알맞은 것을 써넣으시오.\exprbox{\begin{minipage}{0.96\linewidth}{\small\bfseries 증명}\par\smallskip\centering $\begin{aligned} {}_{n-1}\mathrm{C}_r+{}_{n-1}\mathrm{C}_{r-1} &= \dfrac{(n-1)!}{r!\mkern3mu \boxed{\text{㈎}}}+\dfrac{(n-1)!}{\boxed{\text{㈏}}\mkern3mu (n-r)!} \\ &= \dfrac{(n-r)\cdot(n-1)!}{r!(n-r)!}+\dfrac{r\cdot(n-1)!}{r!(n-r)!} \\ &= \dfrac{\boxed{\text{㈐}}\mkern3mu (n-1)!}{r!(n-r)!} \\ &= \dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}={}_n\mathrm{C}_r \end{aligned}$\par\smallskip $\therefore\ {}_n\mathrm{C}_r={}_{n-1}\mathrm{C}_r+{}_{n-1}\mathrm{C}_{r-1}$\end{minipage}}
