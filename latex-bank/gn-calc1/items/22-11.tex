두 함수 $f(x)$, $g(x)$에 대하여 $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1} f(x)=3$, $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1} g(x)=-1$일 때, 다음 극한값을 구하시오.
\dmsubprob{1} $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1}\{f(x)+g(x)\}$
\dmsubprob{2} $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1}\{f(x)-2g(x)\}$
\dmsubprob{3} $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1} f(x)g(x)$
\dmsubprob{4} $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1}\{g(x)\}^2$
\dmsubprob{5} $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1}\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}$
\dmsubprob{6} $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 1}\dfrac{2f(x)+3g(x)}{\{f(x)\}^2}$
