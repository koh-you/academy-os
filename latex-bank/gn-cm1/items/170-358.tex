\dmsubprob{1} $\dfrac{1}{\alpha}+\dfrac{1}{\beta}+\dfrac{1}{\gamma}$
\dmsubprob{2} $(\alpha+\beta)(\beta+\gamma)(\gamma+\alpha)$
\dmsubprob{3} $\dfrac{\gamma}{\alpha\beta}+\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta\gamma}+\dfrac{\beta}{\gamma\alpha}$
