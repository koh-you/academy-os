**문제**

직각삼각형 ABC (빗변 $c$, 둘레 10). 빗변 길이 $c$의 범위 빈칸 채우기. (가) $f(c)$, (나) $g(c)$, (다) $k$. $\dfrac{k}{25} \times f(\tfrac{9}{2}) \times g(\tfrac{9}{2})$. [4점]

**답**: ② $11(\sqrt 2 - 1)$

**풀이**: $a + b = 10 - c = f(c)$. $ab = 50 - 10c = g(c)$. $D \ge 0$ → $c \ge 10(\sqrt 2 - 1) = k$.

$\tfrac{k}{25} \cdot f(9/2) \cdot g(9/2) = \tfrac{10(\sqrt 2 - 1)}{25} \cdot \tfrac{11}{2} \cdot 5 = 11(\sqrt 2 - 1)$.
