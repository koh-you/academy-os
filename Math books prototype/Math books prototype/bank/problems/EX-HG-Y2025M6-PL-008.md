**문제**

$k - \dfrac{3}{k} = 6$일 때, $k^3 - \dfrac{27}{k^3}$의 값을 구하시오. [3점]

**답**: 270

**풀이**: $\left(k - \tfrac{3}{k}\right)^3 = k^3 - \tfrac{27}{k^3} - 9\left(k - \tfrac{3}{k}\right)$.

$k^3 - \tfrac{27}{k^3} = 6^3 + 9 \cdot 6 = 216 + 54 = 270$.
