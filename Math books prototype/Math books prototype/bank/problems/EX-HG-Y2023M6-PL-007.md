**문제**

이상 기체 $V = R\left(\dfrac{nT}{P}\right)$. 용기 $A$, $B$에서 $n_A = \tfrac{n_B}{4}$, $P_A = \tfrac{3 P_B}{2}$, $T$ 같음. $\dfrac{V_A}{V_B}$의 값은? [4점]

① $\tfrac{1}{6}$ ② $\tfrac{1}{3}$ ③ $\tfrac{1}{2}$ ④ $\tfrac{2}{3}$ ⑤ $\tfrac{5}{6}$

**답**: ① $\tfrac{1}{6}$

**풀이**: $\dfrac{V_A}{V_B} = \dfrac{n_A / P_A}{n_B / P_B} = \dfrac{1/4}{3/2} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.
