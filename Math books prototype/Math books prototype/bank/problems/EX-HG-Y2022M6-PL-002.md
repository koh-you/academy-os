**문제**

다항식 $x^3 + x^2 + x + 1$을 $2x - 1$로 나눈 나머지는? [2점]

① $\tfrac{9}{8}$ ② $\tfrac{11}{8}$ ③ $\tfrac{13}{8}$ ④ $\tfrac{15}{8}$ ⑤ $\tfrac{17}{8}$

**답**: ④ $\tfrac{15}{8}$

**풀이**: $P(1/2) = \tfrac{1}{8} + \tfrac{1}{4} + \tfrac{1}{2} + 1 = \tfrac{15}{8}$.
