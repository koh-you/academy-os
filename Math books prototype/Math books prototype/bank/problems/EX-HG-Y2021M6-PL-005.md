**문제**

$x = -2 + 3i$, $y = 2 + 3i$일 때, $x^3 + x^2 y - xy^2 - y^3$의 값은? (단, $i = \sqrt{-1}$) [3점]

① $144$ ② $150$ ③ $156$ ④ $162$ ⑤ $168$

**답**: ① $144$

**풀이**: $x^3 + x^2 y - xy^2 - y^3 = x^2(x+y) - y^2(x+y) = (x+y)(x-y)(x+y) = (x+y)^2 (x-y)$. $x + y = 6i$, $x - y = -4$. $(6i)^2 (-4) = -36 \cdot (-4) = 144$.
