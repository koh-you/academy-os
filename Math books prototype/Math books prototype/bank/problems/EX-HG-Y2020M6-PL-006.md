**문제**

$x - y = 2$, $x^3 - y^3 = 12$일 때, $xy$의 값은? [3점]

① $\dfrac{1}{3}$ ② $\dfrac{2}{3}$ ③ $1$ ④ $\dfrac{4}{3}$ ⑤ $\dfrac{5}{3}$

**답**: ② $\dfrac{2}{3}$ ($x^3 - y^3 = (x-y)^3 + 3xy(x-y) = 8 + 6xy = 12$, $xy = \dfrac{2}{3}$)
